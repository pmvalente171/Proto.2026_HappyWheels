# Movement

1. Q and E are left and right pedals respectively. They should be represented by a bar that moves left or right. Completing a rotation in the pedals means moving the bar fully back and forth.
2. Left and right arrow keys are used for steering. The steering wheel should rotate left or right based on the input.
3. W and S are for moving going up or down a gear. Decreasing the gear reduces the size of the bar, while increasing the gear increases the size of the bar.
